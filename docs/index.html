Search first.
Plug in second.

Storecat catalogues every drive you own, then finds any file in milliseconds whether the drive is plugged in or sitting in a drawer. It shows where your space goes and moves files between drives without ever losing one.

Download for free Source code
winget install Storecat.Storecat

Find anything

Names, paths, tags, photo and audio metadata, text inside documents and files inside archives and disc images.

See it all

Every drive with its type, connection, health and free space, and a treemap of what fills it.

Move safely

Copies are hashed and verified before an original is touched, and every job survives a crash.

Keep it private

Everything stays on your computer. Open source under the GPL.

Built for a shelf full of drives

Plug a drive in once. From then on Storecat knows what is on it.

Search every drive at once

Results appear as you type, from drives that are connected and drives that aren't. When a file is offline, Storecat tells you which drive to fetch.

kind:videosize:>1gbmodified:2023drive:seagatecontent:"invoice"tag:client-x

See where the space went

A treemap of each drive, coloured by what fills it. Click a block to open the folder. Free space is split into fast and spinning storage so you know where new work should live.

Suggestions you approve

Storecat notices what you'd want to know and turns each finding into a job you can review:

  • a drive reporting a health warning needs a rescue copy
  • a year-old project is taking up the SSD
  • Desktop and Downloads need a sweep
  • the same 40 GB lives on three drives

Nothing moves until you press start.

Know your drives

SSD, NVMe or spinning disk. USB, SATA or network. Model, health and when you last saw it. Give each drive a role and Storecat knows where archives and backups belong.

And the small things

The details that make a catalogue worth keeping.

Quick search anywhere

Ctrl+Shift+Space opens a search bar over any app from the tray.

Duplicates across drives

Found by quick hash, confirmed by full BLAKE3 before anything is recycled.

Network shares and discs

NAS shares, mapped drives, DVDs and ISO images. An offline share never stalls the app.

Catalogue travels with the drive

A copy is written to each drive so another computer can import it.

Command line too

Scan, search, tree, suggest and backup from a terminal with storecat.

Updates you control

Signed updates from GitHub, checked once a day, installed when you click.
